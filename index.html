01

Capital One × Discover, M&A Analysis

IB Analyst · M&A

A fully modelled case study on the real Capital One and Discover Financial Services merger, an all stock transaction announced at $35.3bn in February 2024 that closed at an actual $51.8bn in May 2025 once the acquirer's share price had moved. The build works through the exchange ratio, run rate synergies of roughly $2.7bn by 2027, and a full adjusted versus GAAP EPS accretion bridge that reconciles to management's own guidance of double digit accretion in 2026 rising past 15% in 2027. A separate risk register scores nine regulatory and credit concentration risks, including the roughly $250bn of combined card receivables the deal concentrates rather than diversifies.

02

Klarna IPO, Valuation Model

IB Analyst · Equity Research

A three statement build and valuation triangulation for Klarna's actual initial public offering, priced at $40 a share for a $15.1bn valuation in September 2025 after peaking near $46bn in 2021 and troughing at $6.7bn in 2022. The model decomposes that swing into a revenue growth effect and a multiple re-rating effect, then cross-checks a discounted cash flow estimate near $24 a share against a comparable company range of 3.0 to 5.5 times forward revenue built from Affirm, PayPal, Block and Adyen. Stress grids flex funding cost, credit loss rate and discount rate assumptions before landing on the actual $15.1bn offer and $19.65bn first day close.

03

Portfolio Risk Engine

Market Risk Analyst

A Value at Risk engine that runs Historical, Parametric and Monte Carlo methods side by side, the Monte Carlo leg using a Cholesky decomposition to preserve cross asset correlation. GARCH(1,1) volatility forecasting feeds the risk estimates, and every model is backtested with the Kupiec and Christoffersen tests against the Basel traffic light framework rather than reported and assumed correct. A Streamlit dashboard makes the pipeline usable for live, multi asset risk reporting rather than a one off notebook.

04

Credit Risk & Bankruptcy Analysis

Credit Risk Analyst

A forward looking credit assessment of PG&E Corporation, built ahead of its 2019 bankruptcy filing so the conclusions could later be checked against what actually happened. Three independent frameworks, the Altman Z-Score, the Ohlson O-Score and a Merton structural model, are triangulated across leverage, liquidity and default risk indicators rather than relying on a single score. All three converged on a bankruptcy probability above 50%, flagging the default before it occurred rather than explaining it afterward.

05

Statistical Arbitrage Strategy

Quant Researcher · Market Risk

A cointegration based pairs trading pipeline built on 128 large and mid cap US equities across 20 years and 76 rolling walk forward windows, using Johansen tests, Ornstein-Uhlenbeck spread modelling and Kalman filtered hedge ratios. The strategy nets a 0.68 Sharpe ratio after transaction costs, though the write up is upfront that a survivorship bias correction likely brings that closer to 0.34 to 0.47, and it reports a capacity frontier showing the edge stays viable only up to roughly $10 to $30 million in assets under management. Extensions like dynamic hedging and multi asset baskets are tested honestly against the baseline, including the case where they make results worse.

0.68 Sharpe, net of costs128 equities, 20 years~$10 to $30M capacity

06

Reactive Limit Order Book Simulator

Market Risk Analyst · ML Engineer

A queue aware limit order book simulator combining a C++ matching engine with Hawkes process calibration and Monte Carlo execution modelling, built to test whether a more realistic model of the order queue actually improves execution predictions rather than just fitting the data better. Calibrated on real LOBSTER tick data and validated against real, order ID level reconstructed fills, it found the calibrated model wins on fill timing accuracy but not on fill probability accuracy at the sample size tested, and it reports that mixed result rather than the more flattering half of it. The C++ engine runs about 58 times faster than the Python reference implementation in isolation.

07

Volatility Surface Forecasting & Options Strategy

Data Scientist · ML Engineer

Two deep learning architectures, an LSTM with attention and a ConvLSTM, trained on 26 years of daily SPX options data to forecast the full implied volatility surface across ten maturities and eighteen deltas. Rather than stopping at forecast accuracy, the forecasts are wired directly into a delta hedged short strangle strategy with VIX based entry gates and real transaction costs. The ConvLSTM driven strategy returned 31.3% annualised with a 1.37 Sharpe ratio in a 2004 to 2022 backtest, ahead of a 24.0% and 0.96 Sharpe rule based benchmark.

Financial Research Copilot
